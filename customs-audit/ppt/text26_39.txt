Additional opportunities identified in one or more countries
Fines and Penalties Import (Policy Section 2.6)
• Lack of review and action plans for customs penalties
Recommendation:
• Ensure full implementation of Regional F&P Control process: Implement root cause analysis of fines and penalties and take actions to mitigate the risk of
  potential penalties
• Perform consistent feedback to Operations/Customs team on fines and penalties incurred to convey consequence of operational errors, promoting awareness
  and corrective actions with monthly follow ups.
• As results of the root cause analysis, identify opportunities for training and reinforce the procedures with the staff involved in the operation.




Import/Export High risk goods Clearance (Policy Section 4.3/4.12)
• No country process in place to define scope and complete review of import/export high-risk goods
• Export declarations of high-risk goods not always reviewed by at least two qualified employees prior to the submission to customs authorities
Recommendation:
• Implement a process to ensure proper review of high-risk goods for imports and exports as outlined in the Policy.
